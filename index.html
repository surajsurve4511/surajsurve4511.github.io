MANAS Humanoid — Full-Body Robot with LLM Cognition & Digital Twin

● ACTIVE
RoboticsAI/ML Generative AIHardwareDigital Twin

Building classroom robots is standard. Building a full-body humanoid that processes Indic speech → reasons through a Qwen 4B LLM → executes autonomous tasks while maintaining a bidirectional Isaac Sim digital twin across 70 links is an architectural nightmare. Here is how MANAS was born.

⚠ Bottleneck 01 — Human-Robot Interaction at Scale

Humanoid robots need to understand natural language in Indian regional languages.

The Problem: Standard speech-to-text fails with Indian accents and regional dialects. The robot needs to understand intent, reason, and respond naturally.

The Engine: Full HRI pipeline: Indic STT → Qwen 4B LLM (running locally via GGUF) for intent extraction and reasoning → Natural TTS response. The robot autonomously plans and executes tasks in showroom and university environments.

⚠ Bottleneck 02 — 70-Link Kinematic Synchronization

Simulating 70 links in real-time across ROS2 and Unity is computationally explosive.

The Problem: The MANAS upper body has 70 links, 69 joints (54 moving), requiring bidirectional state sync between the physical robot, ROS2, and the Unity 6 visualization studio.

The Engine: High-throughput 60+ Hz UDP telemetry schema v2.0 bridging ROS2 ↔ Unity 6. TwinPhi studio with 26 independent drive sliders, 28 mimic joints, and 16 preset conversational gestures.

⚠ Bottleneck 03 — Multi-Camera Motion Capture

Optical tracking collapses when the arm is fully extended (collinear singularity).

The Problem: SVD DLT triangulation across calibrated camera arrays breaks when shoulder-elbow-wrist become collinear — exactly the pose most needed for pointing gestures.

The Engine: Decoupled optical-inertial swing-twist filter: optical rays determine arm pointing vector, BNO085 IMU quaternions resolve axial roll/twist. Separate concerns, eliminate singularity.

6-DOF AI Robotic Arm — Bridging LLM Intent with Physical Kinematics

● ACTIVE
RoboticsAI/ML Generative AIHardware

"Pick the red cube" — translating plain English into sub-centimeter, collision-free physical movement while maintaining a real-time Digital Twin is the bridge between natural language hallucination and precise physical kinematics.

⚠ Bottleneck 01 — The Translation Gap

LLMs generate text. Servo motors require joint angles.

The Problem: No native path from a language model's probabilistic output to deterministic joint-angle commands.

The Engine: "Brain Node" — Ollama LLM extracts intent → YOLO World detects object in live feed → 3D coordinates computed → MoveIt calculates inverse kinematics + collision-free trajectory → physical execution.

⚠ Bottleneck 02 — Sim-to-Real Synchronization

Untested AI code destroys ₹8,000+ servo motors.

The Problem: Testing experimental algorithms on physical hardware risks burning out expensive servos.

The Engine: Bidirectional Digital Twin using Gazebo. Every physical movement mirrors in simulation. Every algorithm validated in Twin before deployment to ESP32/Arduino controllers.
